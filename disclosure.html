Affiliate disclosure

This page is the advertising and affiliate disclosure for Family Freeze Guide, published at familyfreezeguide.com.

As an affiliate, we may earn a commission if you click a link on this site and later buy or enroll in a product or service. That commission does not change the price you pay. We are not paid to leave out the free options, and a credit freeze at Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion is free.

How we mark paid links

Affiliate or sponsored links are marked #ad next to the link and use a placeholder or tracking address such as AFFILIATE_LINK_AURA until a live affiliate URL is added. Those links also include rel="sponsored nofollow". If a short video or social post points you to a link in the bio or the About section of this site, and that destination includes an affiliate link, the post should also include #ad.

FTC-style disclosure

The Federal Trade Commission requires advertising to be honest and disclosures to be clear and hard to miss. In plain language:

  • If you follow a link marked #ad and later buy or enroll, we may get paid by that company.
  • That payment is a reason we mention the product. It is not proof that the product is right for you.
  • We do not claim that a paid plan, or a freeze, will stop every scam or every kind of identity theft.
  • Prices and plan terms change. Dated price tables on this site tell you when we checked the company’s official page. Check the official page before you pay.
  • We do not invent official commission rates. If we cannot find a rate on the company’s own public affiliate page, we say so.
