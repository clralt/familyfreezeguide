Privacy

Family Freeze Guide (familyfreezeguide.com) is a static website. In the form published from this folder, it does not ask you to create an account, and it does not include a comment form, a newsletter signup, or a payment page.

What we collect

The files in this folder do not themselves collect names, email addresses, or payment information. If the site is later hosted on a service such as Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages, that host may log standard technical information (for example, IP address, browser type, and the pages requested) according to its privacy policy. We do not control those host logs.

We have not added our own analytics script, advertising pixel, or comment service to these pages. If that changes, this policy should be updated before those tools go live.

Links to other sites

Articles link to official government pages, credit-bureau freeze pages, and, where marked #ad, to a company’s product or affiliate page. Those sites have their own privacy practices. A credit-bureau freeze request is made on the bureau’s site, not on ours, and will require the personal information that bureau asks for. Only use the official addresses linked in the freeze article.

Affiliate links

If you click an affiliate link, the destination company may use cookies or similar tools to credit a later enrollment to this site. That is how a commission, if any, is tracked. See the affiliate disclosure.

Do not send sensitive information here

Do not email a Social Security number, freeze PIN, or bank password to this site. There is no secure inbox attached to these pages for identity-theft recovery. Use IdentityTheft.gov or the official bureau and agency contacts in the articles.

This policy describes the static pages as of November 12, 2026.

Family Freeze Guide is published at familyfreezeguide.com. As an affiliate, we may earn a commission if you click a link marked #ad and later enroll or buy. That does not change the price you pay. A credit freeze at the three nationwide credit bureaus is free and does not require a paid product.
