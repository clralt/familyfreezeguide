## Required disclaimer

Use this exact sentence on any page or script that names Aura:

> No one can prevent all identity theft or monitor all transactions effectively.

## Banned wording

- Do not call Aura “comprehensive” security or “complete” security. “All-in-one digital safety” is the allowed category language in the 2023/2024 guide.
- The Money 2024 award title is “Best Comprehensive Protection.” You may quote that award title as an award title, with the year. Do not reuse the word “comprehensive” as our own description of Aura.
- Do not say a freeze, Aura, or any paid plan will prevent all identity theft, stop every scam, or monitor all transactions effectively.
- Do not invent official commission rates, residuals, or discounts.
- Do not state a savings percentage unless the current official offer supports it, and then only as “Savings up to xx%.” Do not say a flat “Save 50%” (or any other single rate) as if it applied to every plan.

- Do not shorten it to “250X faster” or drop “up to” / “than the competition.” The guide says the proven speed differs by competitor and Aura had not tested against all competitors.

### Free trial (\*\*)

Guide claim: try the product for free; cancel anytime during the free trial. Offer valid for new customers only and can be redeemed only once per customer. Full access depends on identity verification and credit eligibility. After the trial, billed fees auto-renew unless the trial is canceled at least 24 hours before it expires.

### Credit score (¹)

> The score you receive with Aura is provided for educational purposes to help you understand your credit. It is calculated using the information contained in your TransUnion, Equifax or Experian credit file. Lenders use many different credit scoring systems, and the score you receive with Aura is not the same score used by lenders to evaluate your credit.

### 60-day money-back guarantee (²)

Guide claim: annual plans, “try our annual plans risk-free.”

Required terms from the guide:

- Available only for annual plans purchased through Aura’s websites or Aura Customer Support.
- Cancel online or by calling 1-855-712-0021 and request a refund within 60 days of the initial eligible purchase date.
- If the member started with a free trial, the 60 days run from the trial sign-up date.
- A switch to a new annual plan within 60 days of the initial annual subscription may still qualify, based on the initial annual plan purchase date.

Aura’s current App Store and Google Play listings, checked November 27, 2026, also state that the 60-day money-back guarantee **does not apply to purchases made via the App Store or Google Play Store**. See https://www.aura.com/legal/refund-policy. Do not promise the 60-day guarantee on app-store enrollments.

### Family plans (⁴)

Child members on the family plan have access only to online account monitoring and Social Security number monitoring. All adult members get all the listed benefits.
